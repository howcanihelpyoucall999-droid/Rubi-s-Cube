FAQ

Answers at a glance.

How does Dopsbot connect to my devices?
Dopsbot connects via Bluetooth 5.0 to your smartphone or laptop. The companion app (iOS & Android) handles pairing in under 30 seconds. USB-C powers the device.
Does it require an internet connection?
Core features like expressions, focus mode, clock, and games work completely offline. AI assistant features require internet for cloud model access — all personal data stays on-device.
What size is Dopsbot?
Dopsbot is 12cm tall, 8cm wide, and 7cm deep — about the size of a small coffee mug. Designed to sit beside your keyboard without taking up desk space.
Is Dopsbot suitable for children?
Absolutely. Dopsbot is designed for ages 8 and up. Its rounded form and durable casing are child-safe, and parental controls in the app restrict AI and messaging features.
What is the return and warranty policy?
We offer a 30-day no-questions-asked return policy and a 12-month manufacturer warranty. Free replacements are shipped within 5 business days.
